# The Media-Check Checklist

A printable one-pager. Laminate it, fridge it, screenshot it into the group
chat's pinned messages. (Expandable details from the practice scenes are
hidden in print — this page is all signal.)

## Before you believe/share an image

- [ ] I traced it to its first author (or it has none — suspicious)
- [ ] I zoomed 3x on hands and anything they touch
- [ ] I zoomed on all text, logos, and signage in the image
- [ ] I checked reflections and mirrors against the scene
- [ ] I checked that all shadows point in one consistent direction
- [ ] I zoomed on the background for melting/duplicated objects
- [ ] I looked for halos or seams where the subject meets the background
- [ ] I found 2+ tells, so I'm treating it as suspicious (or 0, so it stays innocent)
- [ ] I ran a reverse image search before sharing

## Before you believe/share a voice clip

- [ ] I heard real breathing somewhere in the clip
- [ ] I listened for room sound (echo, hiss, distant life)
- [ ] I checked whether the emotion matches the words
- [ ] I listened at 0.5x speed for flat, metronome-like pauses
- [ ] If it claims to be someone I know, I asked an un-Googleable question
- [ ] I found a second, independent source before calling it real

## Before you believe/share a video

- [ ] I watched the mouth on "m," "b," and "p" sounds for lip-sync drift
- [ ] I looked at hairline, jaw, and ear edges for shimmer/swim
- [ ] I checked glasses reflections (if any) against the room
- [ ] I watched hands and jewelry during gestures
- [ ] I played it at 0.5x speed
- [ ] I asked the two shallowfake questions: when, and where, really?
- [ ] I reverse-searched a screenshot of a distinct keyframe
- [ ] I remembered: short + blurry + shocking = the fake comfort zone

## Before you believe/share a screenshot

- [ ] I searched the exact text in quotation marks
- [ ] I compared the handle character-by-character with the real account
- [ ] I zoomed for pasted-line seams (font weight, baseline, fuzz)
- [ ] I checked the clock math (timestamps vs. status bar vs. calendar)
- [ ] I checked whether "forwarded many times" is showing (travel ≠ truth)
- [ ] I looked for the original post/link, not just the photo of it

## The deciding rules

- [ ] Strong emotion = slower sharing (the inverse lever)
- [ ] "Unresolved" is a legitimate verdict — no shame, no forward
- [ ] Real pixels with a false story get a context correction, not a "FAKE!!"
- [ ] I never accused real media of being AI on one tell alone
- [ ] If money is anywhere in this story, I'm opening *The AI Scam Safety Guide* instead
- [ ] I corrected my own past shares when they turned out wrong
- [ ] I was kind about it — people get fooled because fakes are *good*
