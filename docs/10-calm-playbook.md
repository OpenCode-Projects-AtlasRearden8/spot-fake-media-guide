# 10: The Calm Playbook

Everything in this guide compresses to one loop you can run in about five
minutes from a phone. The goal of the loop is not perfection; it's a settled
mind and an un-spread feed. Here it is, in order, with the time each step
usually takes.

## The five-minute loop

1. **Stop — 10 seconds.** Notice you're moved. Amazement, anger, tears,
   fear: feeling is fine (it's a good sign you're a person); it's just not
   a check. Take one breath. Nothing real needs you to decide in the next
   three minutes, and most false things *cannot* survive a five-minute
   wait.
2. **Trace — up to 60 seconds.** Who made this first? Scroll the caption,
   ask the sharer for the link, look at the account: how old is it, what
   does it do all day, what does the watermark say? A claim with no author
   isn't private — it's unauditable, and that's a fact about the claim.
3. **Zoom — 60 seconds.** For images: chapter 02's three places (hands,
   text, edges). For screenshots: chapter 03's eight checks. For voices:
   chapter 04's listens, starting with breath and pauses. For video:
   chapter 05 — lips on P/B/M, hairline seams, then the 0.5x speed trick.
   Two tells = suspicion, three = act on it.
4. **Search — 90 seconds.** The chapter 06 habit: one keyframe or the whole
   image into a reverse-image search; or the exact claim text in quotes.
   Sort oldest first and read the oldest result.
5. **Label — 30 seconds.** Check for provenance: platform "AI info"
   markers, the "altered content" banners, a Content Credentials chain on
   the original file (chapter 07), a detector's watermark verdict.
6. **Second opinion — 60 seconds.** Hand it to an AI assistant using one of
   chapter 08's recipes, or to the right human — someone who actually
   follows that world's news, or a family member with the passphrase
   (that one's chapter 04 territory).
7. **Decide — 30 seconds.** See below. That's the loop: about five minutes,
   and it collapses to 60 seconds with practice.

## The four verdicts (and what each one means you do)

| Verdict | What it means | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Real** | The check found good provenance, a credible source, or nothing wrong anywhere you looked | Share it — with its source named. That's the whole upgrade: *verified* sharing instead of blind sharing |
| **Fake** | Tells found, source traced to an AI tool, dated/attributed contradicting the story | Don't share. Consider the kind correction (chapter 09, Script 2), or just leave it |
| **Unresolved** | The checks were ambiguous, the original is unreachable, sources are thin | **Don't share.** Say "can't tell / waiting for sources" if you reply. Unresolved is a full, legitimate destination |
| **Real pixels, false context** | The media is real but labeled with a wrong date/place/cause (the shallowfake) | Correct the *context* — "this is real, but from [earlier place/day]"; chapter 06's oldest-match finds this one |

Notice that in three of four verdicts, the move is *share less* than you
would have. That's not pessimism; that's the whole design: it's easier to
never have to undo.

## What to do with a fake you've found

- **In a group chat:** Script 1 or 2 from chapter 09 — gentle question, or
  kind correction with the link that proves it.
- **At the platform:** most sites have a "report" or "AI content" flag;
  use it for the ones that matter (that's how the labels in chapter 07 get
  better). Ten seconds, and it compounds.
- **In your own history:** if *you* shared it earlier, correcting your own
  forward ("correction: the photo above was AI") is a tax the whole internet
  needs a bit more of, and it buys back your credibility at face value.
- **If money got involved anywhere:** stop reading this manual right there
  and open *The AI Scam Safety Guide* instead — its chapter 08 begins the
  recovery path, starting with the bank.

## The low-energy version (for real life)

Some days and some people don't have five minutes — and of course many
messages shouldn't get them. The reduced kit:

- The **one-habit version:** reverse image search. Just that. It catches
  the most fakes of any single move you can make.
- The **10-second version:** trace. Who's the original author of this?
  Unattributed big claims are allowed to expire.
- The **zero-check version:** feel it, don't forward it. You're allowed to
  love a thing without streaming it to 47 chats.
